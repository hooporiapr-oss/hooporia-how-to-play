RECOGNITION
Watch. Watch Again. Spot the Change.
👁️ WATCH ORIGINAL
📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
Watch the original sequence — cells light up one at a time, gold.
2
Watch the echo — the same sequence again, purple this time, but ONE cell is different.
3
Tap the cell that changed — from memory, on a blank grid with no hints shown.
⭐ Progression
→
Starts at 3 cells in the sequence — every correct round adds one more.
→
A correct answer earns an escalation bonus on top of the base score — the longer the sequence, the bigger the bonus.
→
Fixed at 100 BPM — the pace stays constant, only the sequence length grows.
🎮 Game vs. Training
🏀
Game — the real, escalating run. Starts at 3 cells and keeps growing until you miss.
🏋️
Training — pick a fixed level (1–5) and complete 10 rounds at that exact length to practice.
💡 Tip
The answer never gets shown until you've genuinely used up your chances — a wrong tap in standalone play opens up to two REPEAT windows at a reduced score, and only reveals the real answer once those are gone. In the tracked Full Game, a miss ends that quarter immediately.
