👁️ WATCH THE SEQUENCE
📖 The Playbook ▼
🎯 Objective
1
Watch the cells light up one at a time, in sequence — each with its own note, unlike Recall's all-at-once flash.
2
Remember the exact order they lit up in.
3
Tap the cells back in that same order before the beat runs out.
⭐ Progression
→
Starts at 3 cells — every correct round adds one more.
→
A correct sequence earns an escalation bonus on top of the base score — the longer it grows, the bigger the bonus.
→
Fixed at 100 BPM — the pace stays constant, only the sequence length grows.
🎮 Game vs. Training
🏀
Game — the real, escalating run. Starts at 3 cells and keeps growing until you miss.
🏋️
Training — pick a fixed level (1–5) and complete 10 rounds at that exact length to practice.
💡 Tip
A wrong tap doesn't end the round outright in standalone play — you get up to two chances to hit REPEAT and try again, at a reduced score. In the tracked Full Game, a miss ends that quarter immediately.
